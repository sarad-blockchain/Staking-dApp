Process

How It Works

A simple, transparent staking protocol built on Ethereum smart contracts. No intermediaries, no custody risk — your tokens, your rules.

01

Connect Your Wallet

Use MetaMask or any EIP-1193 compatible wallet. Your private keys never leave your device — we only request a read connection to detect your address and balances.

MetaMask supported
WalletConnect compatible
No sign-up required
02

Approve Token Spending

Before staking, you grant the smart contract permission to transfer your tokens. This is a standard ERC-20 approval — you set the exact amount the contract is allowed to move.

One-time approval
Exact amount authorized
Revocable anytime
03

Stake the Fixed Amount

Deposit the exact configured amount into the vault. The smart contract enforces this strictly — no partial deposits are accepted. Your tokens are locked in a non-custodial vault.

Fixed amount enforced on-chain
Non-custodial vault
Immutable lock logic
04

Wait for the Lock Period

Your stake is locked for a fixed period defined in the contract. During this time, rewards accrue. The progress bar in your dashboard shows exactly how far along you are in the cycle.

Real-time progress tracking
Transparent on-chain timer
No hidden fees during lock
05

Claim Your ETH Reward

Once the lock period ends, claim your ETH reward with a single transaction. The reward is sent directly to your wallet — no wrapping, no extra steps, no waiting for batches.

Direct ETH transfer
No withdrawal queue
Tokens stay in vault until withdrawn
06

Restake or Withdraw

After claiming, your tokens remain staked and the cycle resets automatically, or you can withdraw them to your wallet at any time. Compounding is as simple as staying staked.

Auto-cycle on claim
Withdraw anytime post-lock
No re-approval needed
Non-custodial & Transparent
The smart contract code is open source and verifiable on-chain. No admin key can move your tokens. You can withdraw independently of any frontend.
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SkyStake staking protocol.

What is SkyStake and how does it work?
SkyStake is a non-custodial Ethereum staking protocol. You deposit a fixed amount of tokens into a smart contract vault, wait for the lock period to complete, and receive an ETH reward directly to your wallet. The entire process is governed by immutable on-chain code — no team or admin can interfere with your funds.
Is my stake safe? Who controls my tokens?
Your tokens are held in a smart contract, not by any individual or company. The contract logic is fully on-chain and verifiable. There is no admin key or upgrade mechanism that could move your stake without your action. You can verify the contract address directly on Etherscan at any time.
Why is the staking amount fixed?
The fixed amount simplifies the reward distribution math and ensures fairness. Every staker deposits the same amount and earns the same ETH reward per period. This design prevents whale manipulation and makes the protocol predictable and auditable by anyone.
What happens if I don't claim my reward in time?
Your reward stays available indefinitely after the lock period ends. There is no expiration on claiming — you can claim it a day later or a month later. Your staked tokens also remain safely locked until you explicitly withdraw them.
Can I withdraw my tokens before the lock period ends?
No — the lock period is enforced at the smart contract level. Early withdrawal is not possible during an active staking period. This protects reward integrity and ensures the protocol remains sustainable. Once the period ends, you can withdraw freely.
What wallet do I need to use SkyStake?
SkyStake works with any EIP-1193 compatible wallet — MetaMask is the most common choice. Hardware wallets (Ledger, Trezor) connected via MetaMask are also supported. Make sure your wallet is connected to the correct Ethereum network before interacting with the contract.
Are there any fees beyond Ethereum gas?
No protocol fees are charged beyond standard Ethereum gas costs. The gas fee is paid to Ethereum validators for processing your transaction — SkyStake does not take a cut. The ETH reward you receive is the full amount configured in the contract.
What does "approve token spending" mean?
ERC-20 tokens require an explicit approval step before any contract can move them. When you click "Approve", you're authorizing the staking contract to transfer the fixed staking amount from your wallet. This is a standard and well-understood Ethereum pattern — you can revoke the approval at any time through tools like Etherscan or Revoke.cash.
